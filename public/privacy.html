Privacy

This service stores the email, name, avatar, mood, room messages, room events, watch history, queue items, and mini-game state needed to run each room.

Rooms are stored on this server. Do not share private, sensitive, financial, medical, or password information in chat.

YouTube videos are played through YouTube embeds. YouTube may collect usage data according to its own policies.

For account help or data deletion, contact the support email listed by the service owner.
